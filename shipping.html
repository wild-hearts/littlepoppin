Shipping

Last updated: 24 June 2026

Where we ship

Digital downloads — everywhere. Nothing is posted; your file arrives the moment you pay, wherever you are in the world.

Art prints, framed prints and canvases — worldwide. Each order is printed by the production partner nearest you rather than shipped from one country, so it arrives in days rather than weeks.

Bamboo swaddles — within Australia only for now. These are parcels we pack and post ourselves, and we do not yet have an international rate we would be comfortable charging. If you would like one sent overseas, get in touch and we will quote you honestly.

Shipping rates

  • Digital downloads: free — there is nothing to post
  • Prints, framed prints & canvases: postage is calculated by destination and shown at checkout before you pay
  • Swaddles within Australia: $9.95 AUD flat, free over $80 AUD

Dispatch & delivery times

Prints are made to order: allow 2–5 business days for printing, plus local delivery — usually 3–7 business days in total, depending on where you are.

Swaddles are dispatched within 1–2 business days, with estimated delivery of 3–7 business days within Australia.

Tracking

Where tracking is available we will email it to you after dispatch.

Contact us on 0456 367 013 or via our contact page and we’ll help.
