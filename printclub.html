Questions

When do my first prints arrive?

Straight away. Your first three prints are emailed the moment you join. After that, three more arrive at the start of each month.

Can I choose which prints I get?

Not yet. Part of the fun is the surprise, and they're picked so each month spans different collections. If there's one you particularly want right now, you can always buy it from the shop for $12.

How do I cancel?

Reply to any Print Club email and we'll cancel it for you right away. There's no lock-in and no cancellation fee, and every print you've already received stays yours.

What if I miss an email?

Each download link stays live for 6 days. If you miss a month, just get in touch and we'll send them again.
