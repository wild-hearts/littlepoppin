Questions

How does an e-card arrive?

You get a private link to send by text, WhatsApp or email. It opens on their phone as a wax-sealed envelope; they tap the seal, the flap lifts and the card opens with your words inside.

What does it cost?

$3 a card, paid securely with Stripe. No account, no subscription.

Can I see it before I pay?

Yes. The editor shows the front and the inside as you type, and “Open it as they will” plays the whole envelope opening.

Can I change the words after sending?

Not after it is paid for: the link they open is the card you wrote. Check the spelling in the preview first. If something is wrong, contact us and we will sort it out.
