Print tips

  • A4, 100%. Every file is A4. In the print dialog choose “Actual size”, not “Fit to page”, so cut lines and cards come out true.
  • Paper. Plain 80 gsm is fine for colouring. Cards, pennants, tags and toppers want 200–250 gsm card so they hold their shape.
  • Ink. Colouring pages are pure black line art and print well in draft mode. Save the good ink for the party sets.
  • Trim. Cut just outside the dotted lines. A ruler and craft knife beats scissors on the straight ones.
  • Sharing. Free for your own family, school bag and party. Please send friends to this page rather than the file; it keeps the library free.

How it works

Are the printables really free?

Yes. Everything in the free library downloads instantly as an A4 PDF, no sign-up, no payment. They are free for personal use: your own family, a classroom treat, a party. Please send friends here rather than forwarding the file.

What exactly do I get with the bigger books?

A print-ready PDF sized for A4, downloaded instantly after payment. Nothing is posted, so there's no shipping cost or wait.

Can I print it more than once?

Yes. Print as many copies as you like for your own family. That's rather the point of a printable. The licence is personal use only, so please don't resell or share the file.

What paper works best?

Ordinary printer paper is fine for colouring pages. For cards, pennants, toppers and tags, white card of 200–250 gsm gives a much nicer result. Then cut just outside the dotted lines.

Is the calendar for a specific year?

No. The day grids are undated on purpose, so the same file works whichever year you print it.
