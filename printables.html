How it works

What exactly do I get?

A print-ready PDF sized for A4, downloaded instantly after payment. Nothing is posted, so there's no shipping cost or wait.

Can I print it more than once?

Yes — print as many copies as you like for your own family. That's rather the point of a printable. The licence is personal use only, so please don't resell or share the file.

What paper works best?

Ordinary printer paper is fine for colouring pages. For the milestone cards, white card of 200–300 gsm gives a much nicer result — then just cut along the outside of each square.

Is the calendar for a specific year?

No — the day grids are undated on purpose, so the same file works whichever year you print it.
