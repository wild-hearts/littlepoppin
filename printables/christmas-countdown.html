Print tips

  • A4, 100%. Every file is A4. In the print dialog choose “Actual size”, not “Fit to page”, so cut lines and cards come out true.
  • Paper. Plain 80 gsm is fine for colouring. Cards, pennants, tags and toppers want 200–250 gsm card so they hold their shape.
  • Ink. Colouring pages are pure black line art and print well in draft mode. Save the good ink for the party sets.
  • Trim. Cut just outside the dotted lines. A ruler and craft knife beats scissors on the straight ones.
  • Sharing. Free for your own family, school bag and party. Please send friends to this page rather than the file; it keeps the library free.
