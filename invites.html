Questions

How do guests receive it?

You get one link. Send it by text, WhatsApp, email or Facebook, or add it to a printed card as a QR code. Guests tap it on their phone, open the envelope and RSVP right there. Nothing to download and no app.

How do I see who is coming?

Every RSVP is emailed to you as it arrives, and your private host page lists every reply with a running count of guests. You get the host link on the thank-you page and by email.

How long is it live?

Twelve months from the day you buy it, so it stays up for the event and well after, for guests who like to look back.

Can I change the details after paying?

Yes. Reply to your confirmation email with the change and it is updated for you at the same link, usually the same day.

Is it better than a Canva or PDF invitation?

A PDF or image cannot count down, open a map, add itself to a calendar or collect RSVPs. A website invitation does all of that at one link, and it looks right on every phone without anyone pinching and zooming.
