Questions

How do guests reply?

They open your link on their phone, break the seal, read the card and tap Yes, No or Maybe. Replies land on your private host page with the headcount, dietary needs and any notes.

What does it cost?

$19 for the event: unlimited guests, replies, the headcount and the guest list. Paid securely with Stripe. No account needed.

Can I change the details after sending?

Yes. Your host page has Edit details; every guest sees the new version at the same link.

Does it work for parents of small children?

That is who it is for. Add a pick-up time in the note, ask about allergies, and let families bring siblings with the plus-ones setting.
